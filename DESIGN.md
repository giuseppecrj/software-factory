# Design

## Operator experience

The factory should feel:
- obvious
- compact
- legible
- calm under failure

## Command design

- Prefer short verbs and predictable flags.
- Make examples copy-pasteable.
- Show next steps after successful commands.
- Show recovery steps after failed commands.

## Artifact design

Every artifact should be:
- readable in raw markdown/json
- scannable in under 30 seconds
- comparable across candidates
- explicit about status and next action

## Scorecards and reviews

- Surface strengths, blockers, and recommendation clearly.
- Do not hide disagreements between reviewers.
- Make winner selection easy for a human operator.
